About Us

ABOUT US

HireMatrix is an intelligent hiring platform that evaluates candidates end-to-end — from resume analysis to interview scoring. Using AI-driven insights, it assesses technical skills, communication, and consistency while ensuring fairness through bias auditing. Our goal is to make hiring faster, more objective, and explainable.

Services

OUR SERVICES

The four pillars of fair hiring — each one runs locally on your data, producing recruiter-friendly reports backed by transparent metrics. Click a tab to jump straight to it, or just scroll.
Service 01
Disparate-impact analysis on any tabular dataset.
// solution.py

Detect Bias

Upload a CSV — we'll flag systemic gender bias overall and inside each occupation, return the EEOC four-fifths score, and tell you whether the disparity is genuine discrimination or just occupational segregation.

Don't have a dataset on hand? Try one of the bundled samples to see Detect Bias in action.

Service 02
Fairness reweighting + CatBoost ranking, by occupation.
// reweight.py · catshortlist.py

Shortlist Candidates

Upload a candidate CSV. We'll first check whether reweighting is necessary, then train a CatBoost ranker. Pick the occupation you're hiring for, set a shortlist size, and get a fairness-audited list with full feature attribution.

Same samples — try them if you want a quick demo run.

Service 03
End-to-end interview pipeline.
// cv_extractor.py · generate_question.py
// transcriptions_pro.py · score.py · aggregrate.py
// Interview pipeline.py

Process Candidates

Walk a single candidate through the full pipeline: parse the resume, generate tailored interview questions, capture answers (audio or text), grade each one with feedback and AI-likeness detection, then assemble a recruiter-ready report.

Capture answers

For each question, upload an audio recording of the candidate's answer (transcribed via transcriptions_pro.py) — or paste a transcript if you don't have audio handy.

Score the session

Each answer is scored on technical accuracy, depth, relevance, clarity and AI-likeness via score.py, then rolled up into a candidate-level verdict by aggregrate.py.

Recruiter report

Print-friendly HTML report assembled by Interview pipeline.py & interview_report.py. Use the buttons on the bottom-right to view or download.

Service 04
Cohort comparison + optional fairness audit.
// fairness.py

Candidate Review

Upload candidate reports (the JSON files saved from Process Candidates, or a CSV with candidate_id, gender, recommendation, overall_score). Compare them on a single dashboard, and optionally run a cohort-level fairness audit using fairness.py.
